# Claims Supported and Not Supported

## Supported by the canonical run

- The cleaned local dataset contains 33,378 records after the documented
  count-based exclusions.
- Candidate toponym, non-toponymic place-description, human-condition,
  textual landscape, strict-interface, and provenance channels are represented
  separately.
- Rule-based channel coverage can be reported as coverage.
- Broad human–environment interfaces form a subset of the human-condition
  records under the revised definition.
- Rule-constrained matching yields a 1,231-record municipality-supported
  subset covering 452 current municipality-like units.
- Municipality support polygons are substantially smaller than prefecture
  support polygons for that subset.
- Polygon-derived proximity summaries change under administrative-resolution
  refinement.
- Prefecture-stratified category–feature associations occur in archive
  language, with reported effect sizes, intervals, permutation values, and
  corrected values.
- Kappa–water and Yurei–death function as construct checks for the extraction
  representation.
- The Kappa representative-point distance diagnostic does not support the
  prespecified closer-to-major-water alternative.

## Not supported by the current data

- Precision, recall, F1, accuracy, or annotator agreement for any extraction
  channel.
- Precision or historical validity of municipality matching.
- Exact narrated-event locations.
- Corrected coordinates or an event-location ground truth.
- Local terrain conditions for prefecture-supported records.
- Physical or ecological relationships between yokai and terrain.
- Historical diffusion from Kyoto.
- Causal effects.
- Population prevalence of folklore by region.
- Independence of records that share a source, municipality, or repeated
  narrative.
- Complete removal of semantic leakage after category-name masking.
