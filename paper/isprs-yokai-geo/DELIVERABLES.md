# Submission package

- `main_submission.tex`: revised ISPRS-format manuscript.
- `main_submission.pdf`: compiled camera-ready candidate.
- `refs_revised.bib`: used-only author--year bibliography.
- `generated_numbers.tex`: analysis-backed numerical macros.
- `generated_table_*_submission.tex`: analysis-backed tables with captions
  below the tabular material.
- `figures/fig1_crown_jewel.pdf` through
  `figures/fig4_archive_language_effects.pdf`: vector figures.

The manuscript presents the scientific representation, equations, and
statistical design without software setup or execution instructions.
It makes
no extraction-accuracy claim without human gold labels.
